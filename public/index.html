Warehouse

First-time setup

Create the administrator account for the warehouse system. You can add other staff afterwards.

Sign in

Type your username and PIN. Without signing in you can still scan an item, but you will only see "Scan recorded".

Forgot your PIN or username?

Ask an administrator. On the Staff screen they press Reset PIN next to your name and give you a temporary PIN. Sign in with it and you will be asked to choose a new one. Your username is also shown on that screen. If you are the only administrator, whoever looks after the server can reset it (see reset-pin.js in the README).
Accounts and data are held on the company server.

Choose a new PIN

Your PIN was reset by an administrator. Type the temporary PIN you were given, then choose your own.
